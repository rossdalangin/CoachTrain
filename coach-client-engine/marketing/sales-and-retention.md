# 📝 High-Ticket Sales Call Checklist

Use this checklist during your strategy sessions to maintain control and increase closing rates.

## 1. Connection & Frame (2 mins)
- [ ] Establish rapport with a "small talk" pattern interrupt.
- [ ] Set the frame: "I'm here to see if I can help you, and if not, I'll point you in the right direction."

## 2. Discovery & Deep Dive (15 mins)
- [ ] **Current State:** "Where are you at right now in your business?"
- [ ] **Desired State:** "Where do you want to be in 12 months?"
- [ ] **The Gap:** "What's the #1 thing holding you back from that goal?"
- [ ] **Cost of Inaction:** "If you change nothing, where will you be in 6 months?"

## 3. Solution Alignment (10 mins)
- [ ] Bridge the gap using your **Grand Slam Offer**.
- [ ] Explain the transformation, not just the features.

## 4. The Close (3 mins)
- [ ] "Ready to get started today?"
- [ ] Handle objections using the **Objection Handling Guide**.

# ♾️ Client Retention & Ascension SOP

Keep your clients longer and increase their lifetime value (LTV).

## Week 1: The "Wow" Phase
- [ ] Automated welcome email within 5 seconds.
- [ ] Personal 1-minute Loom video welcoming them.
- [ ] Immediate access to the **Client Portal**.

## Week 4: The Milestone Check
- [ ] Review their **Onboarding Tasks**.
- [ ] Log a "Milestone Achievement" in the activity history.
- [ ] Ask for a referral or a quick testimonial video.

## Month 3: The Ascension Invitation
- [ ] Review their results against the **Dream Outcome**.
