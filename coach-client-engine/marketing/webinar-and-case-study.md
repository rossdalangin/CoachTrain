# 🎙 Coach Client Engine: The High-Ticket Webinar Script

**Framework:** The "Perfect Webinar" modified for High-Ticket Coaching.
**Goal:** 45-60 minutes ending in a "Strategy Session" (Consultation) booking.

---

## 1. The Introduction (5-10 mins)
- **The Hook:** "In the next 45 minutes, I'm going to show you how to [Dream Outcome] without [Pain Point]."
- **The Big Promise:** "My goal is to give you the exact roadmap to [Primary Goal]."
- **Rule of 1:** "There is only ONE thing you need to know to succeed: [The Key Insight]."

## 2. The Body: The 3 Secrets (30 mins)
- **Secret #1: The New Opportunity.** "Why the old way of [Industry Practice] is broken and what's working now."
- **Secret #2: The Internal Struggle.** "How to overcome the belief that you aren't [Technical/Experienced] enough."
- **Secret #3: The External Struggle.** "How to [Goal] even if you don't have [Resource]."

## 3. The Transition (5 mins)
- "I can't cover everything in 45 minutes, but I've shown you the 'What'. Now, who wants to know the 'How'?"
- "You have two choices: Do it alone (The hard way) or do it with a proven system (The fast way)."

## 4. The Stack & The Call to Action (10 mins)
- **The Pitch:** Introduce your **Grand Slam Offer**.
- **The Call to Action:** "I'm looking for 5 people this week to work with personally. If you want to [Outcome], click the button below to book your Strategy Session."
- **Urgency:** "My calendar fills up fast. If the button is gone, the spots are gone."

---

# 📝 Case Study Interview Guide

Use these questions to extract high-value social proof from your clients.

1. **The Problem:** "What was your biggest frustration before we started working together?"
2. **The Decision:** "What made you decide to join the [Program Name]?"
3. **The Experience:** "What was the 'Aha!' moment during our coaching?"
4. **The Results:** "What specific, measurable results have you achieved since starting?"
5. **The Recommendation:** "What would you say to someone who is on the fence about joining?"
