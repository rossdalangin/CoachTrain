# 🏁 The Coach Engine Launch Checklist

Use this checklist to ensure your business is strategically aligned for high-ticket client acquisition. Check each item as you complete it.

## 💎 Phase 1: The Offer Strategy
- [ ] **Dream Outcome Defined:** Is it a specific result? (e.g., "$10k/mo" not "growth").
- [ ] **Perceived Likelihood:** Have you listed your "Proof" or "Unique Mechanism"?
- [ ] **Time Delay Minimized:** What is the "Quick Win" you deliver in the first 7 days?
- [ ] **Effort & Sacrifice Reduced:** What tedious tasks do you do FOR them?
- [ ] **The Price Anchor:** Is your offer priced at least 5x the cost of the closest "cheap" alternative?

## 🌪 Phase 2: The Acquisition Funnel
- [ ] **Lead Magnet Ready:** Does it solve a specific problem immediately?
- [ ] **Consultation Flow Deployed:** Using the `[cce_funnel]` shortcode.
- [ ] **Qualification Form:** Are you asking at least 3 qualifying questions? (e.g., revenue, urgency, budget).
- [ ] **Show-Up Automation:** Have you enabled the 24h and 1h email reminders?
- [ ] **Tracking Scripts:** Is your Facebook/Google pixel installed in the Step Config?

## 📈 Phase 3: The Sales Machine
- [ ] **CRM Stages Configured:** New -> Contacted -> Booked -> Closed.
- [ ] **Lead Scoring Active:** Do you know who your top 10% hottest leads are?
- [ ] **Sales Playbook Memorized:** Are you diagnosing before you prescribe?
- [ ] **Manual Outreach SOP:** Are you reaching out to new leads within 5 minutes?
- [ ] **Testimonial Wall:** Have you embedded `[cce_testimonials]` on your booking page?

## 🔒 Phase 4: Client Excellence
- [ ] **Portal URL Created:** Is there a private page for your clients to log in?
- [ ] **Welcome Training Uploaded:** Video or PDF explaining the roadmap.
- [ ] **Onboarding Checklist:** Do clients have at least 3 tasks to do immediately after paying?
- [ ] **Resource Library Seeded:** At least 5 high-value assets (scripts, templates, etc).
- [ ] **Success Milestone Set:** What is the "Aha!" moment they need to reach first?
