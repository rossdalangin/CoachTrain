# 💎 Coach Client Engine: The "Grand Slam" Strategic Checklist

Follow this checklist to ensure your Engine is tuned for maximum client acquisition.

## 1. The Offer (Alex Hormozi Framework)
- [ ] **Dream Outcome:** Have you defined a specific, measurable result for your client?
- [ ] **Perceived Likelihood of Achievement:** Do you have case studies or a "Step-by-Step" roadmap visible in your portal?
- [ ] **Time Delay:** What is the "Immediate Win" the client gets within 24 hours of paying?
- [ ] **Effort & Sacrifice:** Have you listed all the things your client *doesn't* have to do because of your service?
- [ ] **The Multiplier:** Have you adjusted your price based on the value, not the hours?

## 2. The Funnel (Russell Brunson Framework)
- [ ] **The Hook:** Does your Lead Magnet headline grab attention in less than 3 seconds?
- [ ] **The Story:** Does your VSL (Video Sales Letter) explain the "Epiphany Bridge" that led you to your solution?
- [ ] **The Offer:** Is the transition from "value" to "pitch" seamless?
- [ ] **The Order Bump:** Do you have a small $27-$97 add-on on your checkout page?
- [ ] **The Upsell:** Do you have a "Fast Track" version of your coaching for people who want results quicker?

## 3. The Automation (The "Engine" Logic)
- [ ] **Immediate Gratification:** Does an email go out the *second* they opt-in?
- [ ] **Stage Transitions:** Are leads automatically moved to "Contacted" when you send them an email?
- [ ] **Booking Reminders:** Are you sending reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before the consultation?
- [ ] **Task Management:** Do you have an automated task to "Follow up" if a lead doesn't book after 48 hours?

## 4. The Portal (Client Experience)
- [ ] **Onboarding Tasks:** Are the first 3 steps for your new client clearly laid out?
- [ ] **Resource Library:** Have you uploaded your core "SOPs" or "Guides" for the client to access?
- [ ] **Activity Log:** Are you logging your coaching sessions so the client sees the progress?

---

## 5. Pre-Built Template Utilization
- [ ] **Templates Selected:** Have you chosen a template from the **Strategic Template Library** (VSL, Webinar, or Challenge)?
- [ ] **Copy Customized:** Have you replaced the placeholder text (like `{{dream_outcome}}`) with your specific offer language?
- [ ] **Automation Linked:** Have you connected your funnel opt-in to the appropriate email indoctrination sequence?
- [ ] **Demo Mode Check:** Have you used the **One-Click Demo Mode** in Settings to visualize how your data will flow before going live?

# 🚀 Sales Strategy: The "Consultant-to-Consultant" Approach

When selling this plugin to other coaches, remember that they aren't buying a plugin; they are buying **Professionalism** and **Freedom**.

### The "Before" (The Pain)
- Chaos of spreadsheets and sticky notes.
- High monthly fees for complex software they don't use.
- Leads falling through the cracks.
- Manual onboarding that takes 2 hours per client.

### The "After" (The Engine)
- A clean, professional dashboard.
- One-time cost (or low monthly fee) on their own domain.
- Automated nurture sequences that work while they sleep.
- Instant, professional client portal that increases perceived value.
